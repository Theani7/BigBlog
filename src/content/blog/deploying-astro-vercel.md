Vercel is the ideal platform for deploying Astro sites. Its edge network, instant rollbacks, and seamless Git integration make deployment effortless.

## Getting Started

Deploying an Astro site to Vercel requires zero configuration. Vercel automatically detects Astro projects and applies the correct build settings.

## Preview Deployments

Every push to a branch creates a preview deployment. This lets you review changes before they reach production.

## Environment Variables

Use Vercel's environment variable management to configure your site for different environments. Never commit secrets to your repository.

## Custom Domains

Vercel makes it easy to connect custom domains with automatic SSL certificate provisioning.

## Monitoring

Vercel provides built-in analytics and logging to help you monitor your site's performance and catch issues early.
